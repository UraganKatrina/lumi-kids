## In short

- Your child's data stays on the phone. We never receive your child's name,
  age or results.
- The app has no ads, no third-party analytics, no chats and no social
  sign-in.
- Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store. The app sends our
  server only the purchase number, to check the subscription.

## Data stored on the phone

A parent enters a child's first name or nickname, birth year and month (no
day) to match activities to age, and a profile picture. The app stores
activity results, the chosen language and the paid subscription's end date.

This data stays on the device and is never sent anywhere. We do not ask for
or store surnames, photos, voice, exact dates of birth, contacts or location.

## What the app sends over the network

1. **New activities.** The app downloads an activity pack from our server.
   The request contains nothing about the child. As with any internet
   request, the server sees the device's IP address; we do not use it to
   identify or locate anyone. [State whether the host keeps request logs
   and for how long.]
2. **Subscription check.** After a purchase the app sends our server the
   subscription ID and the purchase token issued by the store. The server
   checks it with Google or Apple and returns the end date. We do not keep
   this data after replying.

## Purchases

Google Play or the App Store take payment under their own terms; we never
receive card details. Only an adult can subscribe or cancel, in the parent
area behind a parental gate.

## What we don't do

No ads and no advertising ID; no sharing of data with third parties for
advertising or profiling; no sending children's data to AI services; no
external links without the parental gate.

## Deleting data

"Delete all data" in the parent area, or uninstalling the app, removes
everything. A single child's profile can be deleted from its card. Our
server holds no data about children, so there is nothing to delete there.

## Changes

If we start processing new data (for example, parent accounts and sync), we
will update this policy and ask for parental consent first.
